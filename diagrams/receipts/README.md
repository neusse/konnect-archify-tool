# Acceptance receipts

Each diagram has an Archify v3.0.1 `finalize` receipt and a full visual-check
receipt. Finalize receipts prove the validate, transactional-deliver, artifact
check, and browser-check gates. Visual receipts cover light and dark captures at
1440x900 and 2048x1320. Machine-specific roots are replaced with portable
`<tool-repo>` and `<konnect-repo>` markers.

Public screenshots and contact sheets are stored under
`diagrams/evidence/archify-3.0.1/`; raw transactional sidecars remain ignored.
